# Timeline Events

This table lists the six key events currently documented in `timeline.json`.

| Year | Title | Summary |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1765 | Stamp Act | British Parliament imposed direct taxes on the colonies, sparking widespread protests and the rallying cry of 'no taxation without representation.' |
| 1770 | Boston Massacre | British soldiers killed five colonists during a confrontation, inflaming anti-British sentiment throughout the colonies. |
| 1773 | Boston Tea Party | Colonists dumped 342 chests of British tea into Boston Harbor to protest the Tea Act and taxation policies. |
| 1775 | Battles of Lexington and Concord | The first military engagements of the Revolutionary War began when British troops attempted to seize colonial military supplies. |
| 1776 | Declaration of Independence | The Continental Congress formally declared the thirteen colonies independent from British rule on July 4th. |
| 1781 | Battle of Yorktown | American and French forces defeated the British army, effectively ending major combat operations and securing American independence. |

**Source:** `timeline.json` in the repository root.
