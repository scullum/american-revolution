# Historical Sources

Reputable sources for American Revolution history research and verification:

1. **National Archives: Founders Online** (https://founders.archives.gov/)  
   Primary source documents from the Founding Fathers, including correspondence, speeches, and official papers.

2. **Library of Congress: American Revolution and Its Era** (https://www.loc.gov/collections/)  
   Maps, manuscripts, prints, and contemporary accounts from the Revolutionary period.

3. **Journal of the American Revolution** (https://allthingsliberty.com/)  
   Peer-reviewed scholarly articles and essays on Revolutionary War history, published daily by historians and researchers.

These sources provide primary documents, scholarly analysis, and verified historical context for events in the timeline.
